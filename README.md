# My Favorite Movie: Interstellar (2014)

**Interstellar** is a science fiction epic directed by *Christopher Nolan*. Set in a dystopian future where Earth is becoming uninhabitable due to blight and dust storms, the story follows a team of astronauts who travel through a wormhole near Saturn in search of a new home for humanity.

## Why I Love It
* **Emotional Core:** Beneath the massive sci-fi concepts, it is fundamentally a touching story about the bond between a father (Cooper) and his daughter (Murph).
* **Scientific Accuracy:** Physicist Kip Thorne served as an executive producer, ensuring the depiction of black holes (*Gargantua*) and time dilation was grounded in real theoretical physics.
* **Iconic Score:** Hans Zimmer's organ-driven soundtrack elevates every scene, especially the docking sequence.
